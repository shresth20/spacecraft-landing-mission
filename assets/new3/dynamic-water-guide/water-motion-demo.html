Using your actual assets

Open this HTML in a code editor and find const ART. Assign your background and transparent sprite paths there. Land and ship entries have a surface rectangle in source-image pixels. This rectangle, not the entire image including padding or sides, is mapped to the mathematical footprint. Sprite source widths and heights are read after decoding.

The demo does not contain controls, scoring, collisions, or landing validation. In your project, keep those existing systems and feed their approved layout into the decorative layer. The supplied coding-agent prompt describes that integration. Small local SVG opacity/transform loops are used here, but SVG repainting still needs profiling on your target devices. "Fewer effects" removes secondary rings and moving dashed highlights.
